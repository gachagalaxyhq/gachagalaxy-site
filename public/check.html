Sold-price source and sample details

Historical sold-price aggregates are supplied by PokemonPriceTracker and may combine marketplaces. The median, range and count describe the provider's historical sample, not a verified seven-day sample. Last sale and provider update dates are separate; old or small samples are not current valuations.

A price is a record of past sales, not a forecast, an offer, or advice.
