[ Two problems ]

A graded card is only worth what the next platform believes.

Every marketplace keeps its own records and its own prices. Nothing travels with the card.

Copied certs

The same cert can be listed twice.

A slab's cert number can appear on more than one platform, and no single platform can see the others.

Clean CheckA private cross-platform check that will flag a cert held on two platforms.
Siloed value

Each platform prices the card alone.

Value sits inside one marketplace. A buyer elsewhere has no neutral number to trust.

[ How it works ]

Three steps, from marketplace listings to one onchain proof.

Price Oracle, then Clean Check, then Gacha Mark.

01

Price Oracle

Blends asking prices from 8 marketplaces into a fair-value band with a confidence score.

24,542 cards tracked
02

Clean Check

Will run privately: it will flag a cert held on two platforms without exposing either platform's data.

Deploying with Horizen
03

Gacha Mark

Stores the result onchain, keyed by grading authority and cert number. Any platform can read it.

Pre-production on Robinhood Chain

Grading-aware throughout: PSA 9, PSA 10 and CGC 10 are priced separately, because the market prices them separately.
