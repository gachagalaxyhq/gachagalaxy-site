How it works

  1. Send the cert number. Tell us the grader and the number stamped on the slab.
  2. We check and price it. The certificate is verified against the grader's record, and the sold-price range is built from completed sales.
  3. You get the page and the PDF. Usually within one working day. If the certificate does not check out, you are not charged.

Common questions

Is this a token or a digital asset?
No. What you buy is a data service: a verified record of a card's price and certificate, delivered as a web page and a PDF. Nothing is minted, transferred or redeemed, and you do not need a wallet to buy or use it.
Is it investment advice?
No. A Gacha Mark is a record of past sales. It is not a valuation, a forecast, or a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
What if the certificate is wrong?
Then there is no charge. We tell you what we found and you can walk away.
Do you cover graders other than PSA?
Yes, PSA and CGC. Others on request.
