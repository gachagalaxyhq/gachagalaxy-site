Request limits and complimentary allowance

The first-ten-free request allowance is exhausted. It applies across all requests, not per person; existing complimentary allocations are unchanged. New requests are not free, and no payment is collected while payments are inactive. The form shows the latest availability and allowance before you submit. Requests accept PSA and CGC certificate numbers with 6–10 digits. Longer CGC numbers can be looked up in Clean Check but are not accepted by the request form.

What the evidence record describes

  • Cert reference and provenanceA grader and cert reference identify a saved record. Grader records, QR matches and physical authenticity are separate evidence checks; none should be assumed verified. A QR match is not authentication.
  • Price basis and evidence limitsAsking prices stay separate from completed sales. Each record states its own basis; insufficient comparisons do not become a valuation.
  • Publication historyExisting record pages show their saved publication dates, network and evidence history. Reading a saved record is not a new check or a paid order.
  • Existing page and PDF accessReview or export an existing record where available. No new report, delivery deadline or automatic refresh is promised.

Common questions

Is this a token or a digital asset?
The paid product is an evidence data service, not a purchase of a card or a token. Existing saved records may include registry history. Reading them does not transfer ownership or prove custody.
Is it investment advice?
No. A Gacha Mark is not a forecast or a recommendation to buy or sell. Read each record's actual price basis rather than assuming it uses completed sales.
What if the certificate is wrong?
Do not treat a saved cert ref or a QR match as proof of authenticity. Review conflicting evidence by hand. A request does not collect payment or guarantee fulfilment.
Do you cover graders other than PSA?
Clean Check supports saved PSA and CGC evidence. Gacha Mark requests accept PSA and CGC numbers with 6–10 digits; the form confirms current grader availability.
